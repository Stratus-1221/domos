# Changelog

All notable changes to Domos. Newest first.

## 1.2 — 2026-10-30

### Added
- Fractions stack while you type. Tap ÷ (or type `/`) and the term before it becomes the numerator, with the cursor in the denominator. With nothing before it, you get an empty fraction to fill in.
- Backspace at the start of a fraction's top or bottom removes the fraction and keeps what you typed.
- Delete button next to every graph in **My graphs**, with **Undo** in the message that appears.
- This changelog.

### Changed
- "Saved on this phone" is now just **Saved**.
- The version number shows at the bottom of Settings.

## 1.1 — 2026-10-30

### Added
- Exponent box: **a^b** or typing `^` opens a raised box like Desmos. → leaves it.
- Brackets close themselves: `(`, `[`, `{` and every function key (sin, √, ln…). Typing `)` steps over the closing bracket.
- Smarter backspace: an empty box is removed in one press; backspace after a box moves into it.
- Tap anywhere in the line you're editing to place the cursor.

### Changed
- The √ bar stretches over everything under it, while typing and after.
- The keyboard stays open after you delete a line with ✕; the cursor moves to the next line.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-30

First release.
- Graphs: functions, implicit curves, inequalities with shading, polar, parametric, points and lists.
- Sliders with play, draggable points, tables, regressions with r².
- Derivatives, integrals, sums, products, piecewise, list statistics.
- Tap a curve to trace it and see intercepts, turning points and intersections.
- Math keyboard, dark mode, saved graphs, undo and redo, examples.
- Installs as an app and works offline.
